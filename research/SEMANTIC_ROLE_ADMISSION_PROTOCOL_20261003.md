## Source Contract

Declared class names supply the ontology. Every candidate/parent is judged as
direct subtype/appearance, shared material, part, scene co-occurrence, unknown,
or explicit denotation of one declared competitor. Shared/part/scene/unknown
remain neutral. No target-derived definitions or judged per-alias marginals.
Forward and reversed option orders use restored exact next-token letter logits,
not sampled generation. Only agreement on the SAME exclusive competitor admits
a semantic rejection; its strength is the minimum restricted softmax score of
that option across the two orders. These scores are not calibrated correctness
probabilities. This conservative rule cannot certify arbitrary phrase meaning.

Text-only sentinels check explicit wrong-parent roof/wall, canonical building,
shared concrete, part chimney and unknown phrase. Their failure stops prediction
integration, not prompt tuning using target outcomes. All observations, prompts,
order choices and model provenance are retained before prediction evaluation.
