agreement fractions show that this blind spot is frequent in this construction,
not merely a possible counterexample. Changing the later solver cannot create
the missing attachment information.

## What Must Change Before The Next Candidate

Stop the same-source aggregation sweep. The next source must explicitly test
SUPPORT FOR A CLASS, not just support for a phrase or dependency on context.
Its decision unit stays query x alias x rival; an alias may be useful against C
but not against B. No whole-bank hard deletion or survivor quota is justified.

The next feasibility question is whether an independent native CLASS reference,
excluding the tested phrase/semantic family, can corroborate a text-ontology
attachment conflict. Pair-specific action would be allowed only with affirmative
rival support; ambiguity, missing reference and shared ontology meaning stay
unknown. For example roof->wall and roof->building require different ontology
interpretations. Do not use the corruption manifest's declared rival as input
to the selector, or choose a reference cutoff from target masks.

This is not automatically a new contribution. Earlier shared-family ownership
already attempted family-excluded references and underperformed matched
Multiscale on full UDD5/OEM. Earlier Oct2 soft-counterfactual/group readers
also used leave-out references and failed specificity. Those failures stand.
A native, query-resolved reference is a DIFFERENT source to test, not a reason
to rename old ownership or promise it will work. It must add demonstrably useful
information beyond text-only and shuffled references under the same writer.

Before implementing another full reader, specify the exact excluded-family
rule, class-score units, coverage/unknown condition and controls prospectively.
First audit source feasibility on the existing class-name replacement versus
legitimate-paraphrase constructions without loading masks; do not choose words,
group size or thresholds by pixel IoU. Then test complete predictions with
fixed controls. Pure attachment and visual false activation need separate
benchmarks. A source ranking alone cannot promote the final model.

No target-conditioned calibration, seed selection, domain routing or another
Geometry/solver modification follows from these results. A genuine raw-LLM
bank and a post-freeze held-out vocabulary/region remain necessary. Historical
LLM-style files are not verified provider outputs; LoveDA style is identity.
If the new ownership source also fails, abandon the unsupported universal
visual-alias-correctness claim instead of manufacturing a third module.

A full final model needs complete-image/full-eight validation, fair released
VIP and matched20 comparisons, nearest-method Geometry evidence, standalone
cost, foreground/per-class/size metrics and natural-image transfer where
general OVSS is claimed. Current64-window gains do not prove SOTA or CVPR
readiness. The research target is better accountable semantic evidence, not
the requirement that some of20 words must be deleted to tell a paper story.
