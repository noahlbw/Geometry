## Interpretation And Next Research Decision

The existing lightweight model has actionable alias capacity. Concrete
canonical-protected labeled donor actions improve every domain on this developed
panel: local fixed-slot mean48.7393 (+2.0942pp), wide49.9044 (+3.2594pp), and
joint51.8140 (+5.1690pp), compared with baseline46.6450. Joint versus wide also
changes which source(s) receive the action and its total action budget; this is
not proof that an unlabeled joint controller will improve. No oracle value is a
deployable model score or a global accuracy upper bound.

The earlier wide-only controller failures cannot be explained by zero available
benefit in that path: the wide labeled control itself gains3.2594pp. They also
cannot establish that local aliases are harmless. Both paths have useful actions,
and reliable action identification remains missing. The old native/window-only
audit is not substituted for this complete bounded-image evidence.

Survivor renormalization weakens every path's labeled mean. In the frozen local
fields,51.6-54.8% of single removals increase the class score under normalized
pooling; the wide fields show59.6-65.5%. These fractions include padded local
queries and are field diagnostics, not valid-native-pixel frequency. Wide
fixed-slot versus normalized labeled means differ0.8781pp; joint differs2.3683pp.
The comparison isolates the action's normalization choice with the same donor
selection. It does not establish a generally reliable fixed-slot controller:
the earlier fixed-slot GeometryCov candidate already failed.

For wide fixed-slot global single-word removals,896/1159 noncanonical
dataset/protocol/class/word entries have at least one beneficial and one harmful
image-level mIoU change on this panel (numerical sign tolerance1e-9pp, frozen
class support). This is contextual variation in the complete counterfactual
action, not a semantic verdict that each phrase is intrinsically good or bad.
Label-selected per-image single removals reach48.6278 versus47.8547 for a
label-selected per-domain global removal. Neither is deployable or independent.

These are labeled mechanisms available in the action space, not improvements

The DirectWide labeled mean55.0443 exceeds the H-routed wide mean49.9044.
Removing H changes spatial mixing and correction magnitude simultaneously; no
magnitude-matched2H/locality control is present. This result therefore cannot
establish that Geometry writeback is harmful, or justify choosing direct
unconstrained writing as the final model. A signed H is not a nonnegative
probability transport; the existence/fraction of negative entries alone does not
quantify their practical influence.

The next missing contribution is an unlabeled source/action estimate that
distinguishes positive from negative competitive effects, with local and wide
paths accounted for and no survivor mass redistribution hidden in the action.
Do not use these labeled word rankings to choose permanent deletions, tune a
threshold, fit per-domain routing or promote a diagnostic control. Preserve the
existing performance reference and require word-identity controls and frozen
accuracy/cost checks for any later deployable proposal. No new selector is
launched by this audit.
