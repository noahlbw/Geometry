# Frozen Geometry: equivalent reader speed and soft admission

Fixed original Geometry, finite VIP wide observer, all20 aliases, text templates,
native512 local window, original coupling/reconstruction and evaluation mappings.
No parameter fitting or automatic promotion. Original and alternative implementations
are kept separately. This is a developed64 top-left512-window panel, eight per domain,
with unchanged full-image wide context, not full-dataset or independent validation.
Keys are inherited unchanged from the prior RivalFine coupling panel, before masks.

## Arms

- ReferenceHard: untouched reader, used for bitwise score/risk/diagnostic equivalence.
- RivalFineHard_Exact: same rule with crop profiles, margins and stencils cached once.
- NoAdmission_Exact: original coupling, no extra fine observations or admission.
- FineSoft_Weighted: same fine risk r; alias weights 1-r enter normalized log-mean-exp.
  Canonical weight stays1. No binary deletion, quota or fitted strength.
- FineSoft_Excess: same fine risk; suppress only its positive excess softmax responsibility
  above1/K, using the existing soft-writer equation.
- ReuseSoft_Weighted / ReuseSoft_Excess: same respective writer, but the contradictory
  witness comes from already-computed Geometry alias cosine/.07, not extra fine RGB.
  These are new rules, not equivalent acceleration or unchanged unscreened inference.
- ReuseClassMean_Weighted: keep the same per-query/class/rival noncanonical risk sum,
  but distribute it equally over aliases; tests alias specificity versus class calibration.

All risks protect the canonical alias and exclude self/invalid pairs. Directed pair
actions are reconciled by the retained zero-gauge signed potential and reconstructed
through the unchanged Geometry operator. Geometry's local score is not reweighted.

## Measurements

Eight synthetic tests: exact original scores/risks/diagnostics, original stencil/order,
nonunit temperature, zero-risk identity, binary-weight/hard equivalence, prior soft
equation, canonical/gauge invariants and class-mean risk budgets. Then real-checkpoint
bitwise replay on64 windows; historical score replay within1e-4. Scores persist before
masks. A VDD full image additionally verifies all retained full-image predictions and
diagnostics exactly between original and cached evaluators.

Warmed independent single-window pipelines, three synchronized repeats per arm,
alternating execution order; model loading/text encoding/decoding/GT excluded.
Report wide observation, Geometry, fine observation, operator construction, reader and
dense readout separately. Wide/fine stages include their original text/crop preparation,
so are not pure backbone timings. Both resident backbones count in peak memory;
this is a window-context cost, not full-image or total experiment wall time.

Report per-domain/common scored-class mIoU, LoveDA P/D, per-class precision/recall/area,
beneficial/harmful changes and measured speed. Preserve all arms regardless of outcome.
Do not claim every alias useful, globally harmful or novel from this panel alone.
