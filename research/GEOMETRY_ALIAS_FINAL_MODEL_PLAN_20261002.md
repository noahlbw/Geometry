## What The Paper Should Solve

Do not define the task as retaining an attractive number of words. Define it as deciding whether a particular alias provides class-discriminative contextual evidence at a particular location.

1. A valid appearance alias can be useful against B and ambiguous against C. Ambiguity alone is not evidence of harm.
2. A high-response alias can provide correct coverage or steal pixels from a rival. Its response magnitude cannot distinguish the two.
3. A low-response alias can be useful elsewhere. Global deletion destroys this option.
4. Removing an alias from another class changes the denominator and argmax competition even when road's own evidence is unchanged. A road gain can therefore arise from reduced rival false positives or from lost rival true positives; IoU and transition accounting distinguish these cases.
5. Increasing the relative weights of retained aliases can change class calibration independently of semantic quality. Renormalization is an intervention, not a neutral operation.

The desired controller has three states: useful, harmful, and unknown. Preserve useful and unknown evidence; attenuate only supported harmful excess. This is a hypothesis about contextual evidence, not a claim that every alias can be assigned a true global quality label.

## A Two-Module Complete Architecture

```text
Image + fixed candidate aliases + public class names/descriptions
                          |
                Frozen backbone and text encoder
                          |
            Geometry: local logits g, features Y, relation G
                          |
       Existing wide-view alias observations r, with matched views
                          |
       Geometry-conditioned competitive contextual evidence reader
       - class-pair-conditioned alias retention rho
       - unsupported decisions remain unknown, rho=1
       - suppress harmful excess; no forced count or redistribution
                          |
                 corrected observation b_rho
                          |
         existing Geometry-anchored reconstruction -> prediction
```

Module1 is original Geometry. Module2 is one evidence reader coupled to its spatial support and final reconstruction. Alias control is internal to that reader, not a separate dataset-level prompt selector followed by unrelated gates and postprocessing. The original wide observer and numerical reconstruction remain inherited components and must be credited. Calling them new names does not establish originality.

Retain all20 local aliases for the next controlled candidate. This preserves the strong original anchor and measures whether controlling contextual alias evidence helps. It does NOT remove harmful aliases from the local branch, and the paper must not claim complete text denoising from this design.

## Retention Rather Than Weight Redistribution

Keep original profiled logits r_a fixed while computing reliability. For a class with K aliases and temperature beta:

```text
S0 = log(sum_a exp(beta*r_a)) / beta
q_a = exp(beta*r_a) / sum_j exp(beta*r_j)
0 <= rho_a <= 1
S_rho = S0 + log(1 - sum_a (1-rho_a)*max(q_a-1/K, 0)) / beta
```

This is an optional concrete next writer, not validated code or an established original contribution. It suppresses only above-uniform responsibility and does not amplify another alias by renormalizing its weight. The original crop interpolation and overlap assembly must remain unchanged.

Required properties:

- rho=1 exactly recovers the original observer and original coupling.
- Equal profiled evidence produces q=1/K and is invariant to retention.
- Reducing retention cannot increase that class score; good/unknown aliases are not directly amplified.
- The logarithm argument is at least1/K, so the operator is finite.
- Alias permutation changes nothing when evidence and retention are permuted together.
- The harmful-evidence estimator must not depend on its own post-correction prediction.

Important limitations: all aliases can share an equally wrong response, in which case this writer cannot fix it. Good aliases can dominate q and be damaged by a wrong controller. Unequal raw responses and original salience still matter. This operator avoids a calibration pathology; it does not supply semantic truth or guarantee increased mIoU. Do not add count-dependent variants or strengths tuned from the current labeled results.

## Where Reliability Could Come From

The unresolved problem is evidence provenance, not another attention kernel or solver. The next candidate should not reuse local/broad all-alias agreement as its truth source.

A bounded research hypothesis is to require TWO kinds of contradiction before suppression:

1. Class-definition contradiction: compare the tested alias with parent and rival canonical class descriptors derived from public names/definitions, frozen before seeing image labels. Distinguish invalid attachment from merely shared or general descriptions. Descriptors must be released; ontology ambiguity stays unknown. Canonical similarity is fallible, and the failed TextPrior arm already rules out treating it alone as a solution.
2. Image-conditioned rival leakage: test whether the alias's incremental broad evidence is concentrated on Geometry-supported locations that favor the rival under the canonical descriptors, while failing to supply corresponding local/support-preserving evidence for its own class. Do not create positive/negative pools using the tested alias or its nearest synonyms. Use existing paired views first; a Geometry support is not assumed to be a true object mask.

For planning, write rho_a(i,c,d)=1-h_a(i,c,d), with h in[0,1] nonzero only when both contradictions have usable support. Lack of witnesses, conflicting ontology, shared meaning, or unstable evidence sets h=0 and rho=1. No fixed Top-K; no per-class quotas; no increasing weights to compensate for a rejected alias.

The exact mapping from these observations to h is NOT settled by this experiment. It must be specified in a new protocol and checked for nontrivial activity before launching another primary. Do not invent thresholds after seeing the eight-domain scores. Canonical descriptors and separate views reduce direct self-confirmation but share an encoder and can still be jointly wrong; they are not independent ground truth.

Evaluate competitors jointly rather than locking every decision to Geometry's top2. At least include the original local winner, the original broad winner, and their runner-ups before intervention; corrections to a third class can otherwise bypass an A/B controller. Candidate absence remains a limitation. The matched top2 operator should be retained as a control, not silently changed with the writer.

## Geometry's Actual Role And Objective

Geometry supplies locality, retrieval/support coordinates, the original class anchor, and the relation used for reconstruction. It must not certify a coherent wrong class as correct merely because the response is smooth.

The retained reconstruction is exactly:

```text
min_z 0.5*||z-g||^2 + 0.5*||G(z-b_rho)||^2
(I + G^T G)*delta = G^T G*(b_rho-g)
z = g+delta
```

The implementation solves this with at most32 conjugate-gradient iterations. This is a training-free inference objective, not a learned loss. Numerical convergence is not semantic confidence. The local fidelity term limits displacement but does not guarantee preservation of a correct small object; a smooth wrong wide observation can still damage it.

The intended coupling is therefore explicit: Geometry identifies where evidence should be compared, the competitive alias reader changes WHAT contextual evidence is admitted, and the same Geometry relation reconstructs the accepted observation against the untouched local anchor. A new reliability-conditioned reconstruction should not be added until this evidence reader beats its controls; otherwise two simultaneous mechanisms obscure the cause.

## When To Stop Pursuing This Selector

- Below shuffled allocation repeatedly: the current trust signal is not demonstrated; do not conceal it by adding a solver.
- Useful and harmful aliases cannot be separated using the proposed label-free observations: reject that source or statistic, not claim every possible selector is impossible.
- Gains occur only for artificial corruptions, or require damaging valid appearance phrases: restrict the claim to robustness or reject deployment.
- Gains vanish after fixing calibration, matching alias counts/views/background, or measuring standalone inference: the claimed mechanism is unsupported.
- Nearly uniform retention only reproduces the baseline: no genuine selection contribution has been established.

Do not run an unlimited parameter sweep on these labels to obtain a publishable story. If no useful controller emerges, a Geometry-plus-coupling paper with honest all-alias aggregation and vocabulary robustness experiments is stronger than an ineffective extra module. Text expansion itself does not require a selector to be scientifically legitimate.

## CVPR Claim Boundary

The prospective contribution is competitor-conditioned rejection of harmful contextual alias evidence grounded by Geometry, not LLM expansion, VIP's broad visual head, softmax weighting, or conjugate gradient. Its originality remains unverified and requires a focused nearest-method literature comparison.

The paper needs useful allocation beyond shuffling, a nontrivial coupling advantage over simple same-source fusion, raw-vocabulary robustness, error-type evidence for large and small targets, fair complete-VIP comparisons, standalone runtime/memory, and held-out generalization. The present experiment supplies a failure diagnosis and a safe retained model, not these missing claims.
