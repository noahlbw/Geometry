## What We Now Know About Alias Control

1. A useful query-local reference can materially improve contextual correction.
   That information is not interchangeable with a global semantic verdict.
2. The current Qwen role source admits no exclusive conflicts in seven clean
   banks. The new complete model therefore exactly equals reference-only on
   those banks. A source that never activates cannot demonstrate good/bad-word
   separation there. This identity is verified, not inferred from rounded scores.
3. Finer references do not rescue the current extra semantic-attachment rule.
   Its wrong-parent increment remains0.002824pp, while ordinary cosine attachment
   adds0.059200pp. The latter is still a control, not an adopted final selector.
4. Beating hard deletion mostly demonstrates that aggressive deletion can hurt.
   It does not establish that the particular soft selector is useful over no
   alias intervention. A three-module paper story would still be unsupported.
5. Correct meaning, high response and useful margin contribution remain distinct.
   A legitimate vehicle word may fire on road; rejecting only wrong denotation
   does not address that problem.

The original coupling loses0.878789pp from clean to wrong-parent; the new
complete loses0.925690pp. Its higher wrong-parent absolute score is therefore
not proof of reduced vocabulary-noise damage versus original. Source improvements
and screening robustness must be reported separately.

## Retained Two-Mechanism Framework

```text
frozen image/text representations + ontology/candidate aliases
                          |
1. Geometry local reading: descriptors, local anchor g, relation G
                          |
2. Accountable contextual reading and reconstruction
   attributed broad observation and actual alias contributions
   query-local competing-class observations
   contribution admission conditioned on query/alias/rival [unresolved]
   one admitted class field b_star
   z = g + H_G(b_star-g)
                          |
                unchanged dense prediction
```

`H_G=(I+G^T G)^(-1)G^T G` is the classical local-fidelity reconstruction.
G distributes an accepted innovation; it does not establish its semantic truth.
The retained deployment still sets `b_star=b`. Alias admission is internal to
mechanism2, not a global vocabulary TopK stage or an additional independent model.

Original alias responsibility `q=softmax(beta*r)` is already dynamic but is
response, not reliability. The experimental writer attenuates excess responsibility
above1/K and retains UNKNOWN evidence. It does not positively amplify good words
or guarantee A/B adjustments leave A/C predictions unchanged.
