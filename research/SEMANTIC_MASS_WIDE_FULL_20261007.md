## Mechanism evidence and decision

VDD road precision42.7382→43.7964%, recall96.2384→95.9882%; vehicle precision31.9787→33.1796%, recall74.3063→74.0466%. Potsdam car IoU36.7792→37.9182. These gains include fewer false activations, not uniform recall improvement.

VOC motorbike IoU72.0045→62.4687, recall77.5319→67.2691%; car IoU69.6258→64.7523, recall83.2451→75.1504%; person recall81.1687→76.4721%. Sofa IoU65.6431→68.1494 improves. Aeroplane recall95.8508→97.2809% rises but precision66.6904→64.8848% falls. Thus lexical equivalence does not certify interchangeable visual evidence or a safe precision/recall tradeoff. Group salience, amplification and pooling change together; this experiment cannot attribute the losses to just one of them.

Decision: do not promote the semantic-mass aggregation as a universal upgrade; retain it as an exploratory controlled result. Do not enable it only on VDD/Potsdam based on these scores. Existing default task deployment stays unchanged. Qwen-generated variable pools have not been evaluated by this study.

Next mechanism priority: matched local endpoint, wide endpoint and selected coupling with identical words/views/residual preprocessing. Measure coupling's recovered and broken correct pixels by true class to distinguish missing semantic support from harmful transport. Preserve PC's protected residual preprocessing when defining endpoints and label that dependence; do not call this an independent VIP comparison. Endpoint diagnostics are a proposed next experiment, not evidence collected here.
