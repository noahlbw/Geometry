## Fixed Action Space

For original profiled evidence r and q=softmax(beta*r), single-alias full-attenuation MAGNITUDE is D_a=-log(1-max(q_a-1/K,0))/beta; the observation increment is -D_a. Retain the original K and salience; this is suppression-only attenuation, NOT count-renormalized hard deletion.

Let D[j,c]>=0 be maximum existing-writer suppression with every NONCANONICAL alias set to rho0, canonical kept1. Also compute unrestricted D with canonical allowed to be suppressed, as a separate action-space diagnostic. Equal evidence and invalid query coordinates have zero caps. Caps do not require the current semantic-conflict/support source to authorize an intervention: this separates writer capacity from that source's eligibility/ranking. All-original evidence and class logits are unchanged.

The exact existing quadratic reconstruction has H=(I+G^T G)^(-1)G^T G, evaluated in fp64 for audit. Original32-step CG scores and their differences from this exact-system calculation are reported. This numerical audit does not propose a changed inference solver.

For donor/class actions d[j,c] in[-D[j,c],0], at output patch i:

