## Two Core Mechanisms

```text
image + class ontology + candidate aliases
                   |
            frozen image/text encoders
                   |
      +------------+---------------------------+
      |                                        |
1. Geometry local reading              wide contextual observation
fine descriptors Yg                    attributed original VIP observer
local class scores g                   all alias scores and salience
relation G, validity                   exact views/templates/coordinates
      |                                        |
      +--> matched alias contribution witness <-+
                         |
2. accountable contextual admission and reconstruction
query x alias x competing-class reliability; unknown remains neutral
exact bounded alias contribution -> coherent admitted class field b*
Geometry-supported contextual innovation with local fidelity
                         |
              unchanged tile assembly/prediction
```

Alias control is internal to mechanism2. It is not a global TopK vocabulary
filter followed by unrelated confidence gates. Geometry supplies local evidence
and the relation used to write contextual innovation; it is not a semantic oracle.

### Mechanism2: The Actual Admission Contract

At a given wide crop, the original writer has profiled alias evidence
`r_a = template_logit_a * K*softmax(salience)_a` and within-class responsibility
`q_a = softmax(beta*r)_a`. These responsibilities are already dynamic with
location and crop; they do not estimate semantic correctness.

The proposed controller's interface is `gamma(i,a,d) in [0,1]`, attached to a
query, alias and rival. A valid alias can be useful against d yet ambiguous
against another rival. Absent or unsupported evidence is UNKNOWN: gamma0,
not a forced deletion quota or a claim that the alias is correct. Current
canonical protection is an identity safeguard, not proof that canonical words
cannot cause false activation.

Preserve an original-scoring, bounded excess contribution until a better source
is established:

```text
d(c|d) = log(1 - sum_a gamma(i,a,d)*max(q_a-1/K,0))/beta
e(c,d) = d(c|d) - d(d|c)
v_c = sum_d e(c,d)/C
b* = b + v
```

The last two equations are the implemented classical consistent-potential
candidate, not a novel graph solver. Its failed promotion means that it remains
an experimental writer, not the retained model's default. Positive v is relative
competition, not promotion of a beneficial alias. Do not claim all A/B effects
are independent of A/C after multiclass consistency.

The retained local-fidelity reconstruction is:

```text
min_z 0.5*||z-g||_F^2 + 0.5*||G(z-b*)||_F^2
H_G = (I + G^T G)^(-1) G^T G
z = g + H_G(b*-g)
```

G uses the original validity masking/row normalization. This is an inference
objective, not a supervised training loss. Frozen network weights; no optimizer,
target-label loss, domain-specific gain or threshold selection. With no alias
intervention, b*=b and the original coupled model is recovered. With b*=g the
output is g. The residual identity preserves a local anchor but does not prove
small-object boundary correctness; H_G need not have only positive entries.

The current source controls broad aliases only. Keep local20 untouched until a
matched local-source study justifies screening it. Do not claim whole-model
text cleansing from a broad-only controller.

not establish a reliable source, and stricter sign or magnitude cutoffs would
still be motivated by repeatedly observed target labels.

The next evidence contract is query-owned, physically resolved observation,
not another solver or phrase blacklist. Geometry must identify the actual
query's visual support instead of averaging all queries in a128px cell.
The observation must retain native local detail while measuring genuinely
different contextual information. One coarse wide token can cover35.7-146.3

A future source should use comparable unmasked visual observations to separate
alias relevance to the query from generic scene/shared-appearance response.
It must provide both positive evidence and explicit unknown coverage rather
than infer absence or harmfulness from removing most of the image. The source
must be judged at the actual written contribution level and against matched
visual-allocation controls. Query supports or same-encoder agreement alone are
not semantic truth. No such successor witness is implemented or promoted by
this document; its source definition and controls must be fixed before inference.

This requirement is still inside mechanism2. Do not add a detector, region
encoder, pair solver, calibration gate and text filter as independent fixes.
The prior failures show that consistent reconstruction cannot supply missing
semantic information. A complete reliable alias-control model and its CVPR
contribution remain the outstanding objective.

## How This Could Become A Complete Paper Model

Promote only one image-conditioned, label-free reliability rule that improves
actual contextual decisions. Then assemble it inside mechanism2, preserving the
retained Geometry readout and all inference identities. The paper's mechanism
would be Geometry-grounded accountability of contextual semantic contributions,
not '20 words must be screened' or 'more words are always better'.

After development promotion, freeze once and evaluate complete images across
all eight labeled datasets. Report full protocol metrics, LoveDA P/D and D
foreground, UDD5/LandCover.ai foreground, per-class precision/recall/area and
small-object errors. Compare full official VIP fairly, the original Geometry,
the original coupled model and same-source fusion. Historical window screens
are not official-VIP full-dataset comparisons. LandCover.ai replaces iSAID here
because no labeled iSAID validation masks are available.

Use distinct vocabulary stress tests: clean20, public raw-LLM candidates and
predeclared wrong-parent/shared-appearance distractors. The wrong-parent case
tests attachment robustness; it cannot certify recognition of a correctly named
car firing on pavement. Compare all-retained and count-matched random deletion.
The controller may benefit noisy vocabularies without needing to improve every
already-curated20 vocabulary. Report both regimes rather than force a selection
story from an unnecessary clean-vocabulary deletion.

Measure standalone deployed latency/peak memory including every witness forward.
The current64-150.5 masked forwards per window are not an acceptable hidden cost.
Natural-image transfer and a vocabulary not used in development are separate
validation requirements; do not call the repeatedly audited eight domains
untouched validation. Templates may be domain-neutral where appropriate, but
each ablation must match them across methods.

Geometry's nearest-method comparison and an attributed wide observer remain
necessary for the novelty boundary. Classical reconstruction/least squares and
VIP's observation path are not our inventions. A second contribution requires
specificity evidence beyond alias/support/action shuffles, bounded behavior,
noise robustness and independent cost. Performance gains alone do not settle
CVPR novelty or acceptance.
